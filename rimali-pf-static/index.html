Price feed

Paste the published-to-web CSV URL of your Google Sheet. The sheet should have two columns: symbol and price, with prices coming from =GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:"&A2,"price").

How to set up the sheet
  1. Create a Google Sheet. A1: symbol. B1: price.
  2. From A2 down list NSE tickers (AUROPHARMA, CHENNPETRO, …).
  3. In B2 paste =GOOGLEFINANCE("NSE:"&A2,"price") and drag down.
  4. File → Share → Publish to web → choose this sheet → CSV → Publish.
  5. Copy the URL it gives you and paste it above.
